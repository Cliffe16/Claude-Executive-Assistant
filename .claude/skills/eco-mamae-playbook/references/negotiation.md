# 06 – Negotiation & close: move the buyer toward a decision

An objection is often incomplete information. Do not answer everything with a lower price. Clarify what must change for the buyer to decide, then ask for a next step.

## Objection table

| Buyer says | Respond with | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| "Your price is high." | "Which part of the scope seems expensive relative to alternatives?" | Compare like-for-like service; submit approved options. |
| "We have a collector already." | "What would make you consider changing or adding a backup provider?" | Learn contract end date and one known weakness. |
| "Send a profile." | "Of course. What should it help you evaluate – cost, capability or compliance?" | Send the relevant pages and book a review. |
| "No budget this quarter." | "When is your budget review, and what information is needed before then?" | Set nurture date; assess one-off urgent need. |
| "We want same-day startup." | "I can confirm only after dispatch and documentation checks." | Ops sign-off, then confirmed activation date. |
| "Guarantee 100% recycling." | "We can discuss documented recovery services within our verified capabilities." | Confirm material stream and evidence available. |

## Three closes that do not feel pushy

1. **Choice close:** "Which is better for your site: the standard three-day plan or the enhanced schedule?"
2. **Decision close:** "Assuming operations confirms the route, what else does your committee need to approve this?"
3. **Next-step close:** "Can we book 20 minutes with finance and the facility manager on Tuesday to settle the open points?"

## Price-protection principles (from Cases 02, 06, 08)

- Trade scope, don't concede price. If price falls, scope changes transparently.
- Keep a defendable floor; never discount below approved economics.
- Separate revenue from cash: payment terms are part of the deal economics.

## Follow-up rhythm: a guide, not a broadcast campaign

| Moment | Message purpose |
|---|---|
| Same day as site visit | Summary of the problem, missing information, named owner and timing. |
| On proposal delivery | Explain options, confirm receipt and agree the decision date. |
| 1–2 working days before decision | Ask whether any issue could prevent review or approval. |
| After missed decision date | Ask what changed; update the CRM and reschedule only with permission. |
| Long-term nurture | Reach out on a real trigger: handover, occupancy, contract expiry or changed operations. |
