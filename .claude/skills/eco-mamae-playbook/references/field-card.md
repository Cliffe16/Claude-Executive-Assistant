# One-page field card: before you leave the building

Use after every customer-facing meeting. Its purpose is to improve judgment, not to make every customer sound the same.

| Question | If you cannot answer it… |
|---|---|
| What problem does the buyer want solved? | Ask the buyer to describe the operational impact. |
| Who makes the purchase decision? | Name economic buyer and signer; secure a permission-based introduction, not just a caretaker's approval. |
| What is the decision-maker's incentive? | Show genuine organisational benefit and record any commercial concession and its formal approval. |
| What exactly must we collect and how often? | Inspect volume, access and waste classification. |
| Can operations deliver without destabilising routes? | Do not promise dates or windows; obtain dispatch sign-off. |
| Will the economics and payment terms work? | Request commercial review or restructure the offer. |
| What evidence can we truthfully provide? | Only include documented capabilities and authorised references. |
| What is the next action and date? | Agree one before ending the conversation. |

> **The discipline that builds a trusted business:** Sell only what can be delivered. Deliver exactly what was sold. Record what happened. Respond quickly when something goes wrong.
