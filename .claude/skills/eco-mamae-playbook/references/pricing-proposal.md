# 05 – Scope, pricing, proposal: build a quote the company can deliver

The correct price comes **after** scope, operational feasibility and margin review. Sales assembles the facts and proposes options; **only the authorised commercial approver** can sign off pricing and exceptional payment terms.

## The six-line commercial specification

| Item | What the proposal must say |
|---|---|
| Service and waste types | Exact accepted material; exclusions; segregation responsibilities. |
| Schedule | Collection days, time window, holiday rules and escalation contact. |
| Volume & consumables | Bins, monthly bag allowance, expected kilograms or loads; how changes are handled. |
| Evidence | Pickup logs, supporting documents and reporting only where operationally possible. |
| Commercial terms | Recurring monthly charge vs one-off charges, VAT treatment, payment terms, validity and start trigger. |
| Variations | Extra trips, changed waste streams, waiting time, increased occupancy and out-of-scope work. |

## Worked example – illustrative, NOT Eco Mamae's approved tariff

A new apartment complex initially has 120 occupied units, expected to rise to 260. The client requests three pickups weekly plus bags. Instead of quoting at full occupancy, the team prices the initial load and a documented review trigger.

| Assumption | Initial phase | At review |
|---|---|---|
| Occupancy | 120 occupied units | Reassess when 200 units occupied |
| Pickup frequency | 3 days/week, subject to route sign-off | Reassess bags per pickup and frequency |
| Volume evidence | 2-week bin/bag counts and pickup log | Use observed demand, not sales-brochure capacity |
| Commercial mechanism | Defined fixed scope + extra pickup rate | Written revision if scope changes |

**Illustrative trip economics for internal training only:** quote KES 11,500; collection labour KES 2,200; fuel KES 2,800; disposal KES 1,600; bags/equipment KES 400; direct contribution KES 4,500 before vehicle finance, overhead, tax and contingencies.

> ⚠️ This arithmetic is **not** a pricing authorisation or an assertion of current costs. Never reuse these figures in a client-facing quote. Use it only to explain *how* contribution is reasoned about.

## Approval stop-signs (any one → escalate before quoting)

- New region or remote route with material travel time.
- Frequency beyond available capacity or a tight loading window.
- Hazardous, medical or chemically contaminated waste.
- Client requests unusual reporting, recovery claims or penalties.
- Rate reduction, free months, deposit waiver, 60-day credit or unilateral liability terms.
