# 02 – Sales workflow: the nine-gate deal process

A CRM stage describes evidence, not optimism. Never advance a deal merely because a conversation was friendly.

| # | Gate | Rep's job | Move forward only when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Target | Select property, site and fit. | You know the location, property type and likely operational need. |
| 2 | Identify buyer | Map buyer, user, influencer, budget owner, signer and veto holder. | A named final approver or verified introduction path is recorded. |
| 3 | Connect | Secure permission for a short discussion. | The prospect confirms a need, timing or referral to the correct person. |
| 4 | Discover | Ask about current service, pain point, expected relief, waste volume and payment. | You can state the buyer's problem in one sentence. |
| 5 | Inspect | Check pickup point, access, waste streams and frequency. | Operations have enough information to cost and serve. |
| 6 | Design | Choose service specification and options beyond collection and transport. | Ops and commercial approval are obtained where required. |
| 7 | Propose | Send buyer-specific business case and approved offer. | Final approver is copied/invited, or their delegated process is confirmed. |
| 8 | Decide | Agree decision meeting and trade concessions formally. | Authorised approver accepts scope, timing, price and payment in writing. |
| 9 | Mobilise | Transfer details to operations, admin and finance. | A named owner accepts the handover and first collection is scheduled. |

## One conversation, one next action

- **Bad note:** "Interested. Follow up."
- **Good note:** "Facilities manager wants Tue/Thu/Sat pickups before 7 am, dislikes overflowing mixed bins, says finance approves next Tuesday; send two scope options by Monday 11 am and call at 3 pm Tuesday."

## Weekly territory planning

1. **Cluster visits by geography.** Group sites in the CBD, Westlands, Kilimani, Athi River or another viable route area.
2. **Mix immediate and future deals.** Visit occupied sites with live waste pain; schedule nurture dates for projects still under construction.
3. **Allocate preparation time.** Before leaving, know the likely manager, one observation about the site and your desired next step.
4. **Record evidence promptly.** Update the CRM after each conversation, while details are fresh.
