# 07 – Pipeline discipline: make the CRM tell the truth

A rep must be able to open a record and know immediately what the client needs, who decides, what stands in the way and what happens next. Pipeline values must **distinguish recurring monthly services from one-off work**; never combine them into a misleading monthly revenue figure.

## Stage definitions with evidence

| Stage | Minimum proof | Don't confuse with… |
|---|---|---|
| New lead | Site and potential buyer identified. | A qualified opportunity. |
| Contacted | Reachable person engaged or correct introduction obtained. | Decision-maker interested. |
| Discovery / site visit | Need, volume and buyer path captured. | Automatic quotation eligibility. |
| Discussion | Active problem, timing and procurement process known. | A verbal yes. |
| Proposal sent | Approved priced scope delivered to buyer. | A likely win without feedback. |
| Negotiation | Specific terms / objections under active review. | Endless chasing. |
| Won | Written acceptance / PO / contract and agreed scope captured. | Service has started successfully. |
| Lost / nurture | Reason and future trigger recorded. | A deleted contact. |

Special holding states used in the field cases: **Compliance review**, **Ops feasibility hold**, **Disqualified / ethics concern**.

## Writing useful CRM notes

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Met manager. Good meeting." | "Met estate manager; 180/250 units occupied; missed Sat pickups; committee chooses supplier on 12 Nov; send 3-day option and sample log by 5 Nov." |
| "Quotation sent." | "Proposed 3 pickups/wk, bins excluded; emailed facilities + finance 30 Oct; reply due 4 Nov; ops time window approved." |
| "Follow up later." | "Nurture until handover expected January; call property manager 2 weeks before handover and confirm expected occupancy." |

## Weekly sales review – 25 minutes

1. Review newly qualified opportunities: what pain and buying event are verified?
2. Review proposals awaiting decisions: who has a scheduled decision meeting?
3. Review blocked deals: access, credit, price, compliance or route issue?
4. Review wins handed over versus wins activated.
5. Select the three most important next actions for each salesperson.

> **Score what matters:** Track qualified discovery meetings, site assessments, approved proposals, proposal-to-win rate, new recurring revenue, one-off revenue, gross contribution where available, overdue next actions and first-30-day service issues. Do not use raw lead count alone as performance.
