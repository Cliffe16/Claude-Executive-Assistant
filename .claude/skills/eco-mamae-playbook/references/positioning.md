# 01 – Positioning: sell the problem we solve

> **Core belief:** We do not sell waste pickup as a commodity. We sell a reliably managed outcome: clean premises, predictable collections, accountable communication and documented handling.

> **How a good rep thinks:** A promising buyer is not automatically a good deal. You must know: Is there a real problem? Can we serve the route? Who decides? Will they pay? Can we prove the promise?

Our strongest prospects are not simply people who produce waste. They are businesses where missed collections create visible cost: complaints, hygiene issues, interrupted operations, regulatory exposure or management time spent chasing a provider.

## Customer segments

| Segment | What usually hurts | Who decides / what wins |
|---|---|---|
| Occupied apartments & estates | Bins overflow; residents complain of odour, hygiene and dirt; collection timing is inconsistent; dry and wet waste mixed without control. | Estate manager, management company or committee. Win with a workable timetable and complaint protocol. |
| New developments nearing handover | Waste volume is unknown; contractor still on site; incumbent supplier may be retained. | Developer and incoming property manager. Win access early and agree an occupancy trigger, not premature pickup promises. |
| Hotels & hospitality | Early collections, hygiene, guest impression; sacks/bags and reporting matter. | Operations, procurement and finance. Win with reliable access windows, scope detail and credible reports. |
| CBD / commercial buildings | Loading access, collection timing, parking, traffic, security and mixed tenants drive complexity. | Facility manager or landlord. Win with a route and time window the team can actually execute. |
| Factories & construction | Waste composition varies; disposal, vehicle load and compliance can change cost. | Site manager, EHS/procurement. Win with correct classification and a scoped pickup method. |

## 25-second positioning statement

**Say this:**

> "Eco Mamae provides structured waste collection and management for properties and businesses. We assess your site, agree a workable collection plan and give you a clear point of accountability. If your current arrangement is causing complaints or uncertainty, I'd like to understand the problem and see whether we can provide relief."

## Disqualify early when necessary

- **No decision access:** you only have a guard or a receptionist, and nobody will introduce the manager.
- **Unworkable scope:** the requested frequency, vehicle access or waste type has not been approved by operations.
- **Bad economics:** travel, disposal and labour consume the fee, with no workable route-density benefit.
- **Commercial integrity:** personal kickbacks, false paperwork, unexplained cash payments, concealed subcontracting, or unauthorised waste collection/disposal.
- **Payment warning:** the prospect demands credit without approval or will not identify the contracting party.
