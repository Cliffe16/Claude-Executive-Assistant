# 08 – Activation & retention: a contract is not the finish line

Sales owns a clean transfer. Operations should never discover essential commitments for the first time when the truck reaches the gate.

## Mandatory handover sheet

| Field | Required detail |
|---|---|
| Contract & scope | Signed agreement/PO, exact service, price, VAT treatment and exclusions. |
| Site logistics | GPS or detailed address, gate/access, best arrival time, parking, unloading/loading arrangements. |
| Waste details | Accepted streams, estimated bags/bins/tonnage, agreed consumables and unusual material. |
| Route readiness | Vehicle, personnel, permitted receiving facility, contingency and ops sign-off. |
| Contacts | Decision-maker, on-site contact, escalation contact and billing administrator. |
| Billing | Contracting entity, invoice email, eTIMS requirements, payment term, deposit approval. |
| Proof & quality | Collection log, photos with permission, complaint path, reporting schedule. |
| Activation | First collection date and confirmation sent; 7-day check and 30-day review booked. |

## The first 30 days

| When | Sales / service action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Share contact matrix and collection plan; confirm site access and first pickup. |
| Days 1–3 | Check proof of first service; contact client after actual delivery, not before. |
| Day 7 | Ask whether schedule, waste volumes and communications match reality. |
| Day 14 | Review any exceptions, excess bags, extra trips or payment-document issues. |
| Day 30 | Review account health, expected occupancy change and scope adjustments; request a referral only if service is stable. |

## Service incident decision tree

1. Complaint received → acknowledge and create incident
2. → verify with dispatch
3. → communicate a reliable recovery time
4. → complete work and obtain evidence
5. → investigate cause
6. → preventive action and follow-up

The internal target of **fewer than 5% missed/late collections** is a management goal, **not** a contractual guarantee unless formally agreed. Never quote it to a client as a commitment.
