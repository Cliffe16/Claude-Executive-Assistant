# /proposal

Prospect: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read `../eco-mamae-playbook/SKILL.md`, `references/pricing-proposal.md`, `references/buying-committee.md` and `references/negotiation.md`. Read `deals/<slug>.md`.
2. **Readiness check first.** If any of the seven discovery facts is missing, or the buyer path (gate 2) isn't recorded, list the gaps and stop short of a full proposal unless the rep asks to continue with placeholders. If hazardous/chemical/medical waste is involved, do **not** draft a priced proposal – output the compliance-review request (SDS, quantity, packaging, residue, permitted handling route) instead.
3. Output, headed with:

> **DRAFT – requires ops and commercial approval. Not for client release.**

### Six-line commercial specification
The six items (Service & waste types · Schedule · Volume & consumables · Evidence · Commercial terms · Variations). Every price, window or date is a placeholder: `[KES – pending commercial approval]`, `[window – pending dispatch sign-off]`. Recurring monthly and one-off charges on separate lines. Note VAT treatment and eTIMS.

### Two scope options
Option A (base, feasible frequency) and Option B (enhanced) on the **same waste assumptions**, so the buyer chooses scope rather than forcing a hidden price cut. For uncertain volumes (new occupancy), add a documented review trigger like the worked example. Never reuse the playbook's illustrative KES figures.

### Approval stop-signs triggered
Check all five stop-signs; list each one that applies and who must approve it.

### 60-second business case for the approver
Pain · Cost of waiting · Proposed outcome · Two options · Requested decision (with date).

### Recipients & decision meeting
Who should receive it (economic buyer/signatory copied, champion included), and the decision meeting to book.

### Next action
One action · owner · date (usually: submit draft to ops and commercial approver).
