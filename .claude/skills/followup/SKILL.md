# /followup

Request: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read `../eco-mamae-playbook/SKILL.md`, `references/negotiation.md` (follow-up rhythm), `references/buying-committee.md` (three-step cadence, 60-second business case) and `references/field-tools.md` (message templates). Read `deals/<slug>.md` for names, facts and the agreed next step.
2. Pick the message purpose from the rhythm table:
   - **Same-day (after visit):** problem summary, missing information, named owner and timing.
   - **Intro request:** thank the contact, offer the one-page collection review, ask permission to copy them, ask who it should be addressed to.
   - **Proposal delivery:** explain options, confirm receipt, agree the decision date.
   - **Pre-decision (1–2 working days before):** ask whether any issue could prevent review or approval.
   - **Missed decision date:** ask what changed; reschedule only with permission.
   - **Nurture:** only on a real trigger (handover, occupancy, contract expiry, changed operations) – name the trigger.
   - **Cold call (45 s):** a spoken script, not a message.
3. Write it:
   - **WhatsApp:** under ~80 words, plain, polite, one clear ask, no attachments promised that don't exist.
   - **Email:** subject line + short body; copy the right people (champion included, approver copied when appropriate).
   - Professional Kenyan business tone. No prices, dates, windows or incentives unless approved – use `[pending approval]` placeholders. No competitor criticism. No pressure tactics.
4. After the draft: who to send it to, when, and what counts as progress (from the cadence table).
5. **Next action** · owner · date. Never send anything yourself – the rep sends it.
